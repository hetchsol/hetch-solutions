About Hetch Solutions

Hetch Solutions Enterprises Limited is a dynamic and innovative company specializing in the provision of comprehensive solutions in the fields of Information and Communication Technology (ICT), construction, engineering, and mining.

Established as Bits & Bytes Technologies in 2018, it was renamed Hetch Solutions Enterprises Limited in 2023. We have evolved into a dynamic and innovative company specialized in providing a wide range of services in the field of information and communication technology (ICT) particularly in construction, engineering, and mining.
