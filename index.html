About Hetch Solutions

Founded in 2018 as Bits & Bytes Technologies and rebranded in 2023, Hetch Solutions Enterprises Limited has grown into a trusted multidisciplinary partner delivering integrated ICT, Civil Engineering, HVAC and Mining solutions across Zambia.

From server rooms and fiber optic networks to building construction, roads, HVAC systems and surveillance infrastructure, we deliver projects that keep businesses connected, secure and productive. As an NCC Grade 6 Category C registered civil works contractor with EngRB-registered engineers, we serve clients across mining, hospitality, finance, healthcare, diplomatic and government sectors.
